User-authorized real-data diagnostic, not a model-route change or production
batch implementation. Three frozen, authenticated, whole-source-screened
historical windows were reused from settled Luna extractions. Luna answers were
not sent to GPT-OSS. No extracted memories were published.

## Parent inspection and limits

All three inputs were short historical user market-research requests, not a
representative cross-project sample. Saved Luna outputs each described the
request, noted absent completion evidence and proposed one exact cited task.
GPT-OSS returned a completely empty but schema-valid object for the first;
the other two described the request, but none returned a cited proposal. The
third also asserted an environmental web-search limitation that the historical
input did not establish.

This shows fast, inexpensive transport and schema compatibility, **not successful
cited extraction quality**. Empty proposals may be appropriate for transient
requests, so the sample cannot establish general fact/decision recall or model
accuracy. It does expose summary loss and unsupported environmental commentary
under the current prompt. Keep the existing Luna route; do not promote GPT-OSS
from this evidence. Batch transport, retention cleanup, representative coverage
and batch cost remain untested by this diagnostic.
